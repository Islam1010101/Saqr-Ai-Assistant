# EFIPS Children Wing — reading-level evidence pilot

**Date:** 6 November 2026  
**Coverage:** 10 catalogued physical titles from shelf 30 (50 shelf-30 source entries).  
**Status:** **Provisional, source-matched only. Not a signed-off EFIPS edition-level reading assessment.**

## Editorial principle

A title alone cannot establish text difficulty, age suitability, or a student's needs. These first ten entries have independent bibliographic/educational reading-level evidence keyed by *title + author + shelf*, but the actual physical editions in EFIPS have not yet been compared with the referenced edition. The EFIPS catalogue displays **Suggested / Provisional** and a source link, not "verified." All other physical inventory records retain their original classification and do not acquire an invented reading level.

These are broad **EFIPS navigation bands, not Lexile or grade equivalents**:
- **Beginner:** supported picture-based/early-reader text and publisher early-reader level 1 or equivalent
- **Intermediate:** increasing sentence variety, early independent reading level 2, guided/transitional short texts, or illustrated nonfiction requiring more vocabulary
- **Advanced:** longer chapter-based or substantially more complex informational reading requiring greater stamina and inference

Reading age, Lexile, ATOS, GRL and publisher series level are different measures and must not be treated as interchangeable.

## Verification before a librarian-approved label

For each copy, the librarian should:
1. Compare **author, full title, cover, publisher, publication date and ISBN** to the cited edition (especially *Lincoln*, which can have abbreviated title or formats).
2. Inspect representative pages for actual sentence length, vocabulary, typography, illustration support and text density; check the complete length, language and any abridgement.
3. Review emotional and cultural suitability alongside accessibility and required curriculum, independently of reading difficulty.
4. Conduct a short supported reading activity with a teacher or reading specialist and note whether the suggested band is appropriate.
5. Record the *actual copy's ISBN*, review date, reviewer and confirmed tier before changing its status to approved.

**No student-level reading capability is inferred from this table.** A student can benefit from books in more than one band, with or without teacher support. The current browsing pilot never edits ISBN, shelf, author, title, borrowing state or individual student records.

## Remaining work

The rest of shelf 30 (40 original entries), the 268 shelf-22 entries, Arabic children's material in mixed shelves, and other Children's Wing volumes remain unclassified for **reading level**, pending title/edition and text review. The 'children' browsing group previously used shelf labels as a provisional audience; it should not be confused with a verified text-complexity assessment. The full inventory requires a separate physical review and does not become automatically rated from publisher marketing age ranges.
